Professional Hairdressing Scissor Sets

Our sets pair one cutting scissor and one thinning scissor in a matched design, so you get a complete cutting kit in one purchase. Each 2-piece set is 6 to 7 inch Japanese 440C stainless steel and comes with a leather pouch. We also make larger kits with a razor, tweezers and comb, and an 8-piece scissor collection. Our range includes many designs, and we keep adding more.

What is in a set

  • Cutting scissor: two straight blades, used to cut hair to length and shape.
  • Thinning scissor: a toothed blade, used to remove bulk and blend lengths.
  • Leather pouch: to keep your scissors protected.

Why buy a set

Most barbers and hairdressers use both a cutting and a thinning scissor every day. A set gives you two scissors that match in colour and finish, which looks good in a salon or on a barbering station. If you only need a single cutting scissor, see our Barber Scissors.
