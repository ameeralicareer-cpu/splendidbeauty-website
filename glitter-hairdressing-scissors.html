Glitter Hairdressing Scissors UK

Every pair here is a professional-style hair cutting scissor, 6 to 7 inch (the Noir Gold Glitter is 5.5 inch), with a leather pouch included. Which glitter scissors should I choose?

  • Black glitter: Edge, Arc and Curve designs, a dark look with a sparkle that works for barbers and stylists.
  • Gold and amber glitter: Gold Glitter, Arc Amber Glitter and Noir Gold Glitter, a warmer finish.
  • Size: 5.5 inch is the usual salon size, 6 inch is a good all-rounder, and 7 inch suits longer scissor-over-comb work. See our scissor size guide.
