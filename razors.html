Professional Shavette Razors

Our razors are professional shavette razors made of stainless steel, with a replaceable blade. Our range includes many designs and handle styles, and we keep adding more.

What is a shavette razor

A shavette is a straight razor that takes a replaceable blade. Because you change the blade for each client, there is no stropping or honing, which makes it a popular choice for barbers who give shaves and clean neck and edge work.

Handles and finishes

Our razors come with wood, black, coloured and steel handles. Every product page shows 4 photos so you can see the details before you order.

Pouch and warranty

Razors come with a pouch when you order 2 or more razors, or a set. All our products have a warranty, handled over WhatsApp.
