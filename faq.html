Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about our professional barber scissors, ordering, delivery and returns.

What steel are your scissors made from?

Single scissors come in stainless steel (£9.99), Japanese J2 steel (£19.99) and Japanese 440C steel (£39.99). The steel options available are shown on each product page; some designs come in one steel only.

How much do your scissors cost?

Single scissors are £9.99 in stainless steel, £19.99 in Japanese J2 and £39.99 in Japanese 440C. Two-piece sets are £14.99 in stainless steel or £24.99 in J2. Four and five-piece kits are £19.99 in stainless steel. The 8-piece set is £39.99 in stainless steel or £59.99 in J2.

Do you sell left-handed barber scissors?

Please message us on WhatsApp with the design you want and we will confirm what is available. Our guide to left-handed barber scissors explains what to look for.

How do I order?

Tap Enquire on WhatsApp on any product page, or message us on +44 7946 633362. We confirm availability with you, then arrange payment and dispatch.

Do you hold stock in the UK?

Yes, we hold stock in the UK. Message us on WhatsApp to confirm a product is available before you order.

How long does UK delivery take and what does it cost?

UK delivery is free. We send orders with Evri, with tracking, and delivery takes 4 to 5 working days.

Do you deliver internationally?

Yes, we deliver worldwide. International standard delivery is free and stock items take 8 to 10 working days. Premium shipping with DHL or FedEx is available on request.

Can I return my scissors?

Under the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations you may cancel and return your order within 14 days of delivery, without giving a reason. If an item arrives damaged or faulty on our side, we accept the return within 5 to 7 days of delivery. See our returns page for details.

Do your products come with a warranty?

Yes, all our products come with a warranty. Message us on WhatsApp if you have a question about it.

Do you supply barbershops, salons and retailers?

Yes. We offer wholesale with a minimum order quantity on items we hold, and we can add your own branding and packaging. Visit our wholesale page or message us on WhatsApp.
