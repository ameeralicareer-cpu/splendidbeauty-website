How to Choose Barber Scissors UK

Size, steel, handle and tension: what to check before you buy.

The best scissors are the ones that feel right in your hand and suit the work you do every day. Here are the things to check before you choose a pair.

1. Size

Scissor size is measured in inches. Our single scissors come in 5.5, 6, 6.5 and 7 inch. Shorter scissors give more control for detail and short hair, longer ones cover more hair in one stroke. See the full hairdressing scissors size guide.

2. Steel quality

Our scissors are available in three steel qualities: stainless steel, Japanese J2 steel and Japanese 440C steel. Higher grades are generally harder and hold an edge for longer. Price depends on the steel you choose, so ask us for the options on the design you like.

3. Handle and comfort

You may hold your scissors for hours each day, so comfort matters. Look at the finger rings, the finger rest and the shape of the handle in the product photos. Every one of our product pages shows 4 photos, so you can see the design from different sides.

4. Tension screw

The tension screw holds the two blades together and sets how loose or firm they move. Too loose and the blades may fold the hair. Too tight and the scissor feels stiff and tires your hand. Adjust it a little at a time until the blades close smoothly.

5. Cutting, thinning or texturising

Cutting scissors shape and cut hair to length. Thinning and texturising scissors have teeth and remove bulk or add texture. Read more in thinning vs texturising scissors.

6. Single scissor or a set

If you use a cutting and a toothed scissor every day, a matched set can be better value and looks consistent. See the barber scissor set guide.

7. Left or right-handed

If you cut with your left hand, ask us about left-handed scissors. They are made to order for a left-handed grip, see our left-handed scissors guide.

8. Pouch, delivery and warranty

Every scissor and set from Splendid Beauty comes with a leather pouch. We have UK stock with free tracked UK delivery, and all our products have a warranty, handled over WhatsApp. Look after them with our scissor care guide.

Quick checklist

  • Size that suits your usual cuts
  • Steel quality that fits your budget
  • A handle that is comfortable for long days
  • Tension you can adjust smoothly
  • The right type: cutting, thinning or texturising
