Shavette Razor UK: What It Is and How to Choose One

A short guide to barber shavette razors with replaceable blades.

A shavette is a barber razor that looks like a traditional straight razor but takes a replaceable blade. You change the blade for each client instead of sharpening it, which is why shavettes are common in barbershops.

Shavette or straight razor?

  • Shavette: uses a replaceable blade. No stropping or honing, and a fresh blade each time.
  • Traditional straight razor: one fixed blade that has to be stropped and sharpened.

What our shavette razors are

Our shavette razors are made of stainless steel and are approximately 5.5 to 6 inch (14 to 15 cm) long. They take a replaceable blade and come in a wide choice of handle finishes: wood, black, colour and pattern, and steel and metal. Message us on WhatsApp if you need to know which blade type fits.

What to look for

  • Handle and grip: pick a handle that feels secure in your hand. Wood handles have a classic feel, and black or metal handles are easy to wipe clean.
  • Finish: choose the look that matches your shop, from olive wood to matte black and bold patterns.
  • Blade fit: check that the blades you use fit the razor.

Basic safety

  • Use a fresh blade for each client and throw the used blade away safely.
  • Keep your fingers clear of the blade when loading it.
  • Take your time and use light strokes.

Follow your local hygiene rules for blades and tools. If you are new to a shavette, practise before using one on a client.
