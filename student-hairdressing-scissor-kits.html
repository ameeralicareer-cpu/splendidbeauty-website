Student and Apprentice Hairdressing Scissor Kits

Most hairdressing and barbering courses ask students to bring at least one cutting scissor and one thinning scissor, so a matched 2-piece set is a simple place to start. Course requirements differ between colleges, so check your kit list with your tutor before you buy. Our 2-piece sets are 6 to 7 inch, with a leather pouch, from £14.99 in stainless steel.

Which kit suits a student or apprentice?

  • 2-piece set (from £14.99): one cutting scissor and one thinning scissor in a matched design. The simplest starter kit for hairdressing students.
  • 5-piece Gentleman Kit (£19.99): 5.5 inch cutting scissor, thinning scissor, razor, comb and tweezers in a tan leather pouch. A good start for trainee barbers.
  • 8-piece Salon Collection (from £39.99): 4 cutting and 4 thinning scissors in a zip case, for those who want a wider range of scissors in one kit.

Stainless steel or Japanese J2 steel?

Stainless steel (£14.99 for a 2-piece set) is a sensible choice for learning on practice heads. Japanese J2 steel (£24.99) is a step up for regular use. See each product page for the options.
