Barber Scissor Set UK: What Is in Each Kit?

2-piece scissor sets and 4, 5 and 8-piece barber kits explained.

A set gives you scissors in a matched design, so your tools look and feel like a pair. Which one is right depends on whether you only need scissors, or a small grooming kit. Here is what each of our sets and kits contains.

2-piece scissor sets

The most popular starting point. A 2-piece set has one cutting scissor and one thinning scissor in a matching design, so you can set the length and then blend. We have a wide choice of designs, from silver with glitter rings to full gold and black finishes. Most designs are 6 to 7 inch, and one is 5.5 inch. Each set comes with a leather pouch. Not sure what each scissor does? Read thinning vs texturising scissors.

4-piece barber kit

A 4-piece kit has a cutting scissor, a thinning scissor, a razor and tweezers. It suits a barber or home groomer who wants the main tools in one matched kit, with a leather pouch. Our 4-piece kits come in different finishes, including blue, rainbow titanium and black and gold.

5-piece barber kit

The 5-piece kit adds a comb: cutting scissor, thinning scissor, razor, comb and tweezers. Our Gentleman Kit has a 5.5 inch cutting scissor in a black finish with gold accents, and comes in a tan leather pouch.

8-piece scissor set

For salons and academies. The 8-piece Salon Collection has 4 cutting scissors and 4 thinning scissors in a zip case, with mixed handle designs, so one case covers different jobs and the whole team.

Which set should you choose?

  • Starting out or just want scissors: a 2-piece set.
  • Want razor and tweezers too: a 4-piece kit, or a 5-piece if you also want a comb.
  • Equipping a salon or academy: the 8-piece set, or ask us about wholesale barber scissors.
  • Only need one scissor: see our barber and hairdressing scissors.
