Left-Handed Barber Scissors UK

Made to order for a left-handed grip.

If you cut with your left hand, the scissors you use make a real difference. A left-handed pair is built for your grip, so the blades meet cleanly and the scissor feels natural in your hand.

What makes scissors left-handed?

On a true left-handed scissor the blades are set the other way round compared with a right-handed pair, so the cutting edge works properly when held in the left hand. The handle shape is also set for a left-handed grip. A left-handed pair is not simply a right-handed scissor turned over.

Why not use right-handed scissors?

Using right-handed scissors in your left hand can feel awkward. The blades can push the hair apart instead of cutting it cleanly, you can see your cutting line less well, and your hand may tire sooner. Many left-handed barbers and hairdressers prefer a proper left-handed pair for this reason.
