Made in Sialkot

Sialkot, Pakistan, is a city known for making cutlery and scissors for generations. We make our instruments in our own factory there, with 20 years of experience behind us. A team of 20 to 30 people works on our scissors, sets and razors.

We make and sell our own tools. When you order from Splendid Beauty you are buying from the people who make them.

What we make

Barber Scissors Sets & Kits Razors

Steel

Our scissors are made of steel, and our razors are made of stainless steel. The blade engraving on our scissors reads Splendid Beauty.

Finishes and details

Our scissors come in many finishes, with coloured screws, glitter rings and engraved handles. Our razors come with wood, black, coloured and steel handles. Our sets pair a cutting scissor with a thinning scissor in matching colours.

Every product page shows 4 photos, so you can see the details before you order.

Every scissor comes with a pouch

Every scissor and every set comes with a leather pouch, even if you order a single piece. Razors come with a pouch when you order 2 or more razors, or a set.

Warranty and support

All our products come with a 1 year warranty, handled over WhatsApp. If something is wrong, message us with your order details and photos. See our Returns, Cancellation & Warranty page.
