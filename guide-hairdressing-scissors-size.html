Hairdressing Scissors Size Guide UK

5.5, 6, 6.5 or 7 inch? How to pick the right size for the cuts you do.

Scissor size is the first thing to get right. A pair that is too short slows you down on longer hair, and a pair that is too long is harder to control on fine detail. Our single barber and hairdressing scissors come in four sizes: 5.5, 6, 6.5 and 7 inch.

6"All-round control
6.5"Blending and longer strokes

How scissors are measured

The size is the overall length in inches, usually measured from the blade tip to the end of the finger rest (the small tang). Brands do not always measure the same way, so check how a size is measured when you compare two makes.

5.5 inch: detail work

The shortest blade gives you the most control. It suits fringes, neckline and fine detail work, short hair and point cutting. It is also comfortable if you have smaller hands. On longer hair you will need more strokes.

6 inch: a good all-round size

Light and easy to control, 6 inch works for most short and medium cuts, including everyday barbering. If you only want one pair to start with, this is a sensible place to begin.

6.5 inch: blending and longer strokes

A little more blade than 6 inch, so each stroke covers more hair. It suits blending, slide cutting and scissor-over-comb work on medium length hair, while still feeling controlled.

7 inch: long hair and larger sections

The longest blade cuts big sections in one stroke, which helps with long hair and one-length cuts. It takes a little more control for small detail, and some people with smaller hands find it long.

Which size is right for you?

  • Think about your usual cuts. Short and detailed work points to 5.5 to 6 inch. Longer lengths and blending point to 6.5 to 7 inch.
  • Think about your hand. Larger hands often like longer scissors, but comfort matters more than any rule.
  • Consider two sizes. Many professionals keep a shorter pair for detail and a longer pair for bigger work.

Left-handed scissors

True left-handed scissors have the blades set the other way round, so the cutting edge works properly in a left-handed grip. Using right-handed scissors in your left hand can feel awkward and cut less cleanly. We have left-handed options. Common questions

What size hairdressing scissors do I need? Most people start with 6 inch for all-round work. Choose 5.5 inch for detail and short hair, and 6.5 to 7 inch for blending and long hair.

What size scissors do barbers use? Many barbers use 6 to 7 inch for cutting, and often keep more than one size for different jobs.

Are 7 inch scissors too long? Not necessarily. They suit long hair and large sections, but give less fine control, so many people pair them with a shorter scissor.
