Choosing your scissors

The right scissor depends on the work you do. Most professionals look at three things: size, handle and tension.

  • Size: 6 inch scissors are lighter and easier to control for detail work. 7 inch scissors cut longer sections in one stroke, which suits longer hair and club cuts.
  • Handle: Choose the handle that sits comfortably in your hand through a full day of cutting. Our designs come with different finger rings and handle styles, which you can see on each product page.
  • Tension screw: The tension screw sets how loose or firm the blades move. Many of our designs have a coloured or stone tension screw.

Cutting and thinning scissors

Cutting scissors have two straight blades and are used to cut hair to length and shape. Thinning scissors have a toothed blade and are used to remove bulk and blend lengths. If you want both, see our Sets & Kits, which pair a cutting scissor and a thinning scissor in matching designs.
